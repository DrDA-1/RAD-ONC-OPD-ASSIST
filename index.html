2. Cockcroft-Gault CrCl Equation:

CrCl = [ (140 - Age) × Weight (kg) ] / [ 72 × SCr (mg/dL) ] × (0.85 if Female)

Stage eGFR Range Clinical Category Management Guidance

Standard clinical thresholds for renal drug dose adjustment based on CrCl / eGFR:

CrCl 30 – 50 mL/min Moderate Impairment. Dose reduction required for DOACs, fluoroquinolones, vancomycin, and select chemotherapy.
CrCl 15 – 30 mL/min Severe Impairment. Significant dose reductions or extended dosing intervals needed. Metformin contraindicated or capped.
CrCl < 15 mL/min Kidney Failure / Dialysis. Avoid nephrotoxic agents (NSAIDs, aminoglycosides). High toxicity risk for renally cleared medications.

Clinical Guidance & Disclaimer

DA-SP OPD Concurrent Chemo Assist is designed as a point-of-care clinical reference for healthcare professionals. Calculations use standard published clinical equations (Cockcroft-Gault, CKD-EPI 2021, Mosteller, Du Bois BSA, and Calvert formula for Carboplatin).

Clinical Limitations: Serum creatinine-based clearance estimates can under- or overestimate GFR in cases of acute kidney injury (AKI), severe muscle wasting, paraplegia, extreme obesity, or rapid fluid shifts.

Always verify final oncology chemotherapy dosages against institutional protocols and specific oncology guidelines before administration.
